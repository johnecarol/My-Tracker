How it works: balances start from your Oct 13 screenshot. Each logged gain or loss moves that account's balance. Days up to your last logged day with no entry count as $0; days after it use the daily goals. An eval is passed when its balance reaches the target; the funded account then restarts at $0 profit. Minimums: Lucid $1,000 profit; Tradeify $1,500 profit (a $26,500 balance from $25,000). Each account keeps that amount as its floor, withdraws on Fridays after its first full Mon-Fri week at the higher goal, and pays 90%. Log only trading results, not withdrawals. Lower Debt remaining when you pay some of the debt from other income; leave it alone for trading payouts, which the projection already counts. Data is saved to your account when available, otherwise in this browser. Market holidays push dates out a few days.
